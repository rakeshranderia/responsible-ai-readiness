    How the scoring works

    Each question is scored as Yes = 2, Partly = 1, No = 0. The result is intentionally simple:

    • Ready — foundations are broadly in place; continue with proportionate controls and monitoring.
    • Ready with Controls — adoption can proceed selectively, but several foundations need strengthening.
    • Further Assessment Required — material gaps should be addressed before scaling higher-impact AI use.
